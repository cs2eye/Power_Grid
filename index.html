    How a round works
    1. Auction. In turn order, each player may put a plant up for auction. Everyone who hasn't bought yet can bid. You must buy one in round 1.
    2. Resources. Last place buys first. Plants store up to twice what they burn.
    3. Build. Last place builds first. Pay the city slot (10, 15, 20) plus connection costs from your network.
    4. Bureaucracy. Burn fuel to power cities and get paid. The market restocks.

    Turn order: most cities first, ties broken by the biggest plant. The game ends when a network reaches the target size; most cities powered in that last round wins, then money.
